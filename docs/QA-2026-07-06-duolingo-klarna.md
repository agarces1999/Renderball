## Duolingo — overall 3/10, verdict: rebuild

The customer is right, and the problem is not five bad scenes — it is three pipeline failures repeated five times. Every scene sits on a dark navy/brown canvas with orange as the hero color, when Duolingo's brand truth is a bright white canvas dominated by green #57cc02; a Duolingo marketer literally cannot recognize this as their brand. On top of that, the owl logo never rendered (a blank white square sits next to the wordmark in three scenes, and a badly cropped app icon stands in for the mascot in two others), and every scene freezes for its final 30-40% while two scenes are composed as website landing pages complete with nav bars and pagination dots. Because the canvas inversion is baked into the design language that every scene inherits, patching individual scenes won't recover this video — fix the design-language and asset stages, then regenerate all five scenes.

### Systemic causes
- Design-language stage inverted the brand canvas: it produced a dark navy/brown theme with orange #fd9403 as the dominant accent, when the crawled brand truth says white canvas with green #57cc02 dominant — and no gate compared rendered canvas luminance/dominant hue against brand truth, so the inversion shipped into all 5 scenes.
- Logo/mascot asset pipeline has no load-or-blank validation: the owl mark rendered as an empty white rounded square in scenes 0, 2, and 3, and as a sloppily cropped app-icon with visible crop garbage in scenes 1 and 3 — nothing checks that a fetched brand asset actually contains non-blank, correctly cropped pixels before it is composited.
- Build agent front-loads all animation: in every scene the elements pop in during the first half and the final ~2-2.5s is pixel-frozen (plus a mid-scene text reflow in scene 0) — there is no motion rule requiring keyframes to span the scene duration and no frozen-tail detector in QA.
- Build agent defaults to website-hero composition: nav bars, 'LEARN LANGUAGES FREE' links, carousel pagination dots, and bulleted feature lists appear inside rendered video frames (scenes 0 and 3 fully, fragments elsewhere) — the coding agent's landing-page prior is leaking into film scenes with no 'no web chrome' constraint.
- Spec-to-render fidelity is not verified: planned beats degraded silently — face-down quit-book became face-up, the owl 'peek' became a sticker occluding the CHECK button, the green connecting line misses both pills, the white glass dashboard card came out charcoal — meaning the vision QA gate is either not checking scenes against their written concept or its findings are not blocking.

### Ranked fixes
1. RULE (design agent + gate): Lock the scene canvas to the brand background and make the primary brand color (#57cc02) the dominant accent — never let a secondary accent (orange) carry kickers, CTAs, and headlines. Add an automated pre-ship gate that measures rendered canvas luminance and dominant hue against brand truth and blocks on inversion. This single fix addresses the blocker in every scene; all 5 scenes must regen after it lands.
2. RULE (asset pipeline): Validate brand assets at ingest — reject near-uniform/blank images (the white-square 'logo') and detect bad crops (foreign pixels bleeding past edges), and fail the build rather than compositing a placeholder. Fixes the dead-logo blockers in scenes 0/2/3 and the crop-garbage mascot in scenes 1/3.
3. RULE (motion): Require animation keyframes distributed across the full scene duration, add a frozen-tail detector (pixel-diff over the last ~2s of each scene) and a no-mid-scene-text-reflow check to QA. Every scene currently fails this; it is why the video feels unfinished even where content is correct.
4. RULE (composition): Ban web chrome inside video scenes — nav bars, header CTA links, pagination/carousel dots, bulleted feature lists — and prompt the build agent toward brand-film composition. Scenes 0 and 3 are landing-page screenshots today and need full rebuilds under this rule.
5. REGEN (after rules land): Full rebuilds of scenes 0 and 3 (concept also degraded: face-down book inverted, streak flame reads as a water droplet, dashboard card wrong color). Targeted element regens for scene 1 (owl-peek beat occluding the CHECK button, blank opening phone screen), scene 2 (connecting line that misses both pills), and scene 4 (repaint the 'Start learning' CTA green #57cc02, fix card build order so it never shows a blank slab, move the flame emoji off the body copy).

## Klarna — overall 3.8/10, verdict: fix-scenes

The customer is right: the video reads as half-rendered, not badly designed. The story structure, scene order, and brand palette are basically correct (brand fidelity 5-7 everywhere), but every one of the five scenes ships with the same three defects: placeholder data that never resolved (prices showing '$•••.00', a stuck 'Loading' label, a blank white logo box, a tofu glyph), scenes that freeze for their final 2-3 seconds because all motion is front-loaded pop-ins, and compositions that devolve into cluttered dark-mode landing pages with fine-print text instead of motion graphics. On top of that, Klarna's signature pink is nearly absent from the proof scene and the final CTA button rendered purple instead of pink — the single worst brand miss in a Klarna spot. All five scenes need regens, but regenerating without pipeline rules will reproduce the same failures, so fix the pipeline first, then regen.

### Systemic causes
- Placeholder data leaks to final render: the build agent emits masked/dummy values ('$•••.00', '1/4 · $—', 'Loading', empty white logo square, tofu glyph) instead of concrete numbers from the plan, and nothing in the pipeline rejects them — verified in scenes 0, 1, 3, and 4.
- Motion system only does entrance animations: every scene stacks hard pop-ins in the first half then goes pixel-frozen for the last 1.7-3.2s. There is no ambient/idle motion layer, no outro beat, and scene durations are not reconciled with animation-end times — so ~40% of the video's runtime is a still image, including the opening hook and the closing CTA.
- The LEGO element library biases toward web-UI components, so the design agent 'fills' scenes with unplanned stat cards, feature chips, checklists, and body paragraphs. This shrinks the planned hero (badge at 15% width, grid at half-canvas), creates 6-8px unreadable fine print, and produces redundant copy ('Pay in 4' five times in one frame). Scenes compose like static landing pages, not motion graphics.
- No brand-color allocation or contrast enforcement: pink #fea6cc peaks at 0.22% of pixels in the scale scene, the CTA renders purple while a decorative pill gets the pink, and #4a7c7c-on-#0a051c elements are measurably invisible (91-99% near-black pixels). The palette is present but the hierarchy is inverted.
- The QA gate is not comparing built output against the plan's explicit specs: 'a total amount in white', 'badge at substantial size', 'grid filling the canvas', 'pink CTA' were all specified and all missed — meaning plan-to-build drift is currently unmeasured, and placeholder/frozen-frame states pass the vision check.

### Ranked fixes
1. RULE (build agent + gate): Ban placeholder data outright. Every price, stat, and label must be a concrete literal from the scene plan; add a pre-render lint that fails on '•', '—', 'Loading', empty img boxes, and tofu glyphs, and a QA-gate check for masked values. This alone kills the top verified blocker in scenes 0, 1, and 4 and most of the 'looks broken' impression.
2. RULE (motion system): Require full-duration choreography — every scene must have an ambient layer (halo breathing, cursor drift, particle motion) covering 100% of its runtime plus an outro beat, and add an automated frozen-tail detector (pixel-diff over the last 40% of each scene) to the gate. Fixes the dead endings in all five scenes, including the frozen CTA close.
3. RULE (design agent): Motion-graphics composition constraints — one dominant hero element per scene sized per plan, max ~3 content tiers per frame, minimum type size (~24px equivalent at 960px), and no web-UI filler (stat cards / chips / checklists / body paragraphs) unless the plan names them. Then REGEN scenes 2 and 3, whose concepts degraded into landing-page clutter.
4. RULE (brand enforcement): Add a brand-color budget check to the gate — pink #fea6cc must own the CTA and each scene's hero moment, and every text/outline element must pass a contrast threshold against #0a051c. Then REGEN scene 4 (pink CTA, remove dupe copy and phone mockup) and scene 3 (visible teal grid, pink 'Millions').
5. REGEN scenes 0 and 1 against their original plans once rules 1-2 land (real dollar totals, cursor trembling ON the button, pink-hinted ghost pill, resolved payment options; real price with clean strike-through, larger installment slots, fixed logo lockup, remove stray pill) — and have the gate verify each plan-specified hero element actually appears at spec.

## Why the gates passed these builds

No gates were removed — the blocking set (overflow, measure-error, barbell,
cross-piece-overlap) is unchanged and both builds genuinely pass it. The problem
is sensor coverage: every blocking gate measures settled-frame GEOMETRY. Nothing
measures (1) canvas luminance / dominant hue vs brand truth, (2) motion over time
(frozen tails, pop-in-only choreography, mid-scene reflow), (3) data integrity
(placeholder prices, blank logo assets, tofu glyphs), (4) web-chrome leakage
(nav bars, pagination dots inside a film frame). The one gate pointed at this
class — vision QA — silently died on the Duolingo build (now fixed to be loud)
and returned zero findings on Klarna, i.e. its rubric is calibrated for defects,
not for 'is this a good brand film'. The internal stills QA made the same
mistake: single settled max-ink frames hide everything listed above.
